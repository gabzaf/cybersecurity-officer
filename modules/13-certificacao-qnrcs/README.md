# 13 — QNRCS certification scheme

**Keywords:** QNRCS · esquema de certificação · CNCS · ANCC · IPAC · organism of certification · Statement of Applicability · Basic · Substantial · High · ISO/IEC 17065 · national cybersecurity certification

Portugal’s **Quadro Nacional de Referência para a Cibersegurança** has a **certification scheme** (EC QNRCS). CNCS is national cybersecurity certification authority, **scheme owner** and **supervisor**. Certificates are issued by **organismos de certificação** accredited by **IPAC** — not by self-assessment.

This is how an organisation gets a **third-party attestation** against QNRCS, alongside (not instead of) ISO 27001.

---

## Facts I keep straight

- **Voluntary**, unless a specific law makes it mandatory.  
- **No automatic equivalence** from holding ISO 27001 or another scheme.  
- Candidate **must perform a risk analysis** and pick QNRCS measures that treat that risk.  
- Measures: **mandatory** vs **excludable**. Exclusions go in the **Declaração de Aplicabilidade** (statement of applicability) and cannot leave untreated risk.  
- Three **assurance levels:** **Básico**, **Substancial**, **Elevado**. The organisation **declares** the target level; the OC validates. Levels are progressive / cumulative.  
- Audit is **external**. The OC audits, reviews, decides, issues.  
- Certificate validity: **three years**, with surveillance audits and renewal.  
- Audience: public administration, critical operators, OES, DSP, private and NGO orgs established in Portugal that want a recognised maturity signal.

Nonconformities (major / minor) and opportunities for improvement are the audit language. I would prepare evidence against Anexo 1 (audit criteria), not against a marketing PDF.

---

## How this sits next to my other hats

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does QNRCS cert replace Art. 6–8 filings? | No. Legal duties to CNCS still stand. |
| Does it replace ISO 27001? | No. Different criteria, different market signal. Many entities will want both mapped. |
| Who do I call? | An IPAC-accredited OC; CNCS supervises the scheme. |

---

## Interview line

“I can explain QNRCS certification as a CNCS-owned, IPAC-accredited, three-level scheme with a risk-based statement of applicability. I would use it as assurance to stakeholders, never as a substitute for RJSC operational duties.”
