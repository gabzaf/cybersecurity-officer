# 11 — Annual cybersecurity report

**Keywords:** relatório anual de cibersegurança · DL 65/2021 Art. 8 · Regulamento 183/2022 · CNCS · PGP / GPG · Security Officer signature · management review

Periodic evaluation is how you check whether measures worked, what you actually did, and what is still broken. In this legal regime that evaluation is a **Relatório Anual**, signed by the Responsável de Segurança, structured as **Reg. 183/2022** requires, and sent through the instructed channel (in training: **PGP-encrypted** submission to CNCS).

---

## Legal hooks

- **Art. 8 DL 65/2021** — organisations must produce an annual report; the Security Officer signs it.  
- **Art. 6** — the inventory that accompanies the CNCS relationship is signed too — and is **not** the full internal inventory, only **internet-facing** assets ([module 7](../07-inventario-ativos/README.md)).  
- **Art. 5** — if you cannot explain the year, you cannot sign.

This is Portugal’s version of an ISMS **management review**, with a regulator as audience.

---

## What I would put in the year-pack (substance, even if the form is mandated)

- Scope and period  
- Security Officer / PCP identification  
- Summary of **risk analyses** performed and major residual risks  
- Measures implemented vs planned (honest delta)  
- Incidents: count, severity, notifications, lessons  
- Audits and tests (pentest, restore, tabletop)  
- Training / awareness  
- Changes to inventory and internet-facing exposure  
- Objectives for the next cycle  

I practised the **operational** last mile: official template, signing, encrypting to CNCS’s PGP key. A report that cannot be sent in the required form is not done.

---

## Interview line

“The annual report is the Security Officer’s signature on the year. I treat it as management review plus CNCS evidence: what we said we would do, what we did, what broke, what we will do next — encrypted and on time.”
