# 06 — Cybersecurity audit and monitoring

**Keywords:** IT audit · cybersecurity audit · GRC · CIA · control testing · SIEM · continuous monitoring · ISO 27007 · CISA · Art. 5 DL 65/2021

Audit is how the Security Officer finds out whether the SGSI is real. DL 65/2021 Art. 5 puts **accompanying audits** in the role’s orbit — you are not always the auditor, but you own the findings.

---

## Two sentences I do not mix up

- **IT audit:** systematic, independent assessment of processes, systems, infrastructure and controls related to information technology.  
- **Cybersecurity audit:** the same, aimed at **information security** — can the system assure **CIA** of services and of information processed, stored or transmitted.

---

## Scope I would put on an engagement letter

Policies and procedures · cyber infrastructure · access control · incident management · regulatory conformity (RJSC, RGPD, sector rules) · cyber risk management · awareness.

Or, by domain: data security · operational security · network security · system security · physical security.

Monitoring is the continuous cousin: logs, SIEM, vulnerability scans, control KPIs. Audit samples a moment; monitoring is the CCTV.

---

## What I do as Security Officer around an audit

1. Agree **scope and criteria** (QNRCS, ISO 27001 Annex A, CIS IG, internal PSI).  
2. Provide evidence: policies, risk register, inventory, tickets, restore tests — not slides.  
3. Do not coach the evidence into existence the week before.  
4. Track findings to **treatment** (same register as Art. 10).  
5. Report to top management in business language (risk, cost, deadline).

Independence: if I designed the control, I should not be the only one declaring it effective.

---

## Interview line

“I can scope a cyber audit, sit through it without theatre, and turn findings into a risk-treatment backlog with owners. Audit without monitoring is a snapshot; monitoring without audit is unchallenged telemetry.”
