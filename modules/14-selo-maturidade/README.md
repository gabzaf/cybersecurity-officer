# 14 — Digital cybersecurity maturity seal

**Keywords:** Selo de Maturidade Digital · cibersegurança · INCM · IPQ · IPAC · DNP TS 4577 · maturity model · capability · public trust

The **Selo de Maturidade Digital** (cybersecurity dimension) is a **public maturity mark**, in a family of digital-maturity seals under Portuguese quality infrastructure (**INCM**, **IPQ** as national standards body, **IPAC** accreditation, certifying bodies under DNP TS 4577).

It is not the same object as [QNRCS certification](../13-certificacao-qnrcs/README.md) and it is not a substitute for DL 65/2021.

---

## How I position it

| Instrument | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| RJSC / DL 65/2021 | “Are we **legal** as an in-scope entity?” |
| ISO 27001 | “Do we run a **certifiable ISMS**?” |
| QNRCS scheme | “Can an accredited OC attest our **QNRCS measures** at Basic/Substantial/High?” |
| Maturity seal | “Can we show the **market / citizens** a digital-maturity mark in the cyber dimension?” |

I would pursue a seal when the organisation wants a **communicable** maturity signal (procurement, public image, SME roadmap). I would not tell a board the seal closes Art. 10 or incident notification.

---

## Security Officer’s job around a seal

- Map seal requirements to controls we **already** run (avoid a parallel universe of evidence).  
- Treat the audit as another [assurance activity](../06-auditoria/README.md).  
- Keep language honest in marketing: a seal is not “we cannot be breached”.

---

## Interview line

“Maturity seals are a communication and capability tool in the Portuguese quality system. I can run a dual-track: legal RJSC programme plus optional QNRCS cert and/or a digital maturity seal, without mixing the three in a press release.”
